\subsection{Testing, Internal, and Non-Existent \acp{MCC}}

The \ac{ITU} \ac{MCC} assignments reserve certain \acp{MCC} for use in network
testing (MCC 001) and for use in internal or private networks (MCC
999)~\cite{ITU-OB-1156-2018}. Further, \acp{MCC} beginning with ``1'' or ``8''
are not assigned by the \ac{ITU}.  Our data includes many examples of base
stations assigned one of these MCCs.
