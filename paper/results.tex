\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Using the cell identifier collection methodology outlined in
\S\ref{sec:methodology}, we accumulated over 87M unique cell identifiers by
querying Apple's \ac{CPS}. Figure~\ref{fig:cumulative-tech} depicts the
cumulative number of cellular identifiers discovered over time.
